## About Tesla

Tesla, Inc. is a vertically integrated battery electric vehicle (EV) automaker and developer of real-world artificial intelligence software, including autonomous driving technology and humanoid robots. Key facts:

- **Global deliveries (2025):** ~1.64 million vehicles
- **Employees:** ~134,800 (as of Dec 2025)
- **Headquarters:** Austin, Texas
- **IPO:** June 2010 at $17 per share
- **Index:** S&P 500 component
- **Revenue (TTM):** $97.88 Billion
- **Net Income (TTM):** $3.86 Billion

Tesla's product lineup includes luxury and midsize sedans, crossover SUVs, a light truck, and a semi truck. The company also sells battery storage solutions, solar panels, solar roofs, and operates a fast-charging network and auto insurance business.
